# Site reference images

Real imagery from the RHAD EDGE landing page (PSG is now EDGE), the RHAD website and the RHAD45 landing page. Open the ones that match the job **before** choosing or generating imagery, and attach 2 to 3 of them to the ChatGPT prompt as style references (`references/master-image-prompt.md`).

These are references for look and composition. They are not templates to paste into posts, and they are not stock. Do not reuse the people as clients or team members.

## The look in one line

Black-and-white photographic people on a pale or white ground, built up with fragmented grey image tiles, one **thin red thread** with small dark nodes running through the picture, and a **small red cube** as the single colour accent. Calm, editorial, lots of clean space.
