# Web copy for RHAD pages

Copy is a design decision. Write it before layout and show it at checkpoint 3.

## Voice

**RHAD (agency, campaign pages):** confident, commercially focused, plain. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. A named situation, then what RHAD does. "We" for the company. Singapore and British English (enquiry, centre, colour). No hype.

**RHAD45:** calm, precise, concrete. Everyday operational language. One problem, one product, one next step. Write RHAD45 and S$100/month (or SGD 100 as the live page does: confirm) exactly. See `rhad45-web.md`.

## Headlines

- Name the customer's situation or the claim, not the category. "Great businesses shouldn't run on 'I'll remember.'" beats "Smarter customer engagement."
- Two lines or fewer at every width. A full stop is fine.
- One H1 per page. Section headlines carry themselves: no eyebrows, no kickers.
- Do not open every section with a question. Do not tease ("what nobody tells you").
- Say each thing once. The hero says it, the section proves it, the FAQ does not repeat it.

## Calls to action

- One label everywhere, naming the outcome: "Arrange my 45-minute review", "Start a conversation".
- Form submit names the request, not the act: "Request my review". Never "Submit".
- Secondary actions are plain: "See how it works".
- Never "Learn more", "Get started", "Click here", "Unlock".

## Proof and claims

- Real quotes, named, with permission, labelled when they are agency credentials. No ratings or counters without a source.
- Price and grant language is conditional every time: "where eligible", "from", "scope and cost are confirmed before you proceed". Put exclusions next to the price.
- State product boundaries next to the claim they limit.
- Agency results are agency results.

## Forms and microcopy

| Moment | Write |
|---|---|
| Field label | A noun phrase, visible ("Business email") |
| Hint | What helps, short ("We reply to this address") |
| Optional | "(optional)" in the label |
| Error | What happened and what to do ("Enter an email like name@company.com") |
| Pending | "Sending..." on the button |
| Success | What was received and what happens next, only after a confirmed receipt. Never promise a confirmed time unless true |
| Failure | "We could not send this. Your answers are still here. Try again, or message us on WhatsApp." |
| Empty and 404 | Say what is missing and give the next step |

## Words to avoid

revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, next-gen, game-changer, unlock, supercharge, world-class, state-of-the-art, leverage, empower, elevate, synergy, robust, holistic, "AI-powered" as a headline, "in today's fast-paced world", "let me be honest". Em dashes. Exclamation marks (rare).

RHAD45 additionally: guaranteed revenue, never miss a lead, unlimited, fully autonomous, instant bookings, five-star reviews guaranteed, no hidden fees, free setup, cancel anytime, 24/7 support (unless substantiated).

Grant pages additionally: guaranteed, approved vendor, apply now, secure funding, claim grant.

## Reading level and format

Plain words a busy owner reads on a phone. Paragraphs of two or three sentences. Numerals for prices, durations and steps. Lists only for real lists. Alt text says what the image shows and why it is there.

## Market safety

Visitors arrive from Singapore, Indonesia, India, the UK and Australia. Check idioms, currency (S$, Rp, INR, GBP, A$), spelling and humour for each market the brief names. Say which market the page is written for.
