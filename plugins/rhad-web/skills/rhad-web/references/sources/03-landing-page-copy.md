## 5. FAQs

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

### Know what you're signing up for.

**Can RHAD45 work with the systems we already use?**

We check that first. We review the relevant accounts and workflows you choose to share, then confirm the supported connections, the chosen scope and what your team needs to approve before setup.

**What's included in S$100/month?**

Any one RHAD45 product, with ongoing monitoring and support within the agreed scope. Setup, WhatsApp/API usage and third-party software are charged separately. You don't need all four products. If you want more than one, we'll confirm the scope and pricing before you proceed.

**What happens in the 45-minute business review?**

Akash and Ashwin review your goals, current accounts and customer journey with you. Together, you'll identify where automation could help, which product is relevant and what needs to be scoped. If SEO, social media or marketing strategy deserves attention, those services are discussed separately.
