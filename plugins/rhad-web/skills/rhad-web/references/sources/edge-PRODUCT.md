# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, one folder per campaign page (`/edge/` at the root, `/rhad45/` in `rhad45/`). Shared assets in `assets/`. Google Fonts for Unbounded and Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Users

Owners and managers of Singapore SMEs who run sales and service themselves. They lose leads and repeat business to follow-up that depends on someone remembering: a WhatsApp enquiry nobody answered, a customer nobody checked in with, a review nobody asked for, numbers spread across tabs.

## Product Purpose

RHAD (RHAD Pte Ltd, a Singapore digital agency) runs campaign landing pages that lead to a consultation. Two live products:

- **EDGE grant packages** (`index.html`): fixed-scope digital marketing packages for EDGE-eligible SMEs.
- **RHAD45** (`rhad45/`): a suite of four automation modules: WhatsApp Lead Response, Loyalty Loop, Review Engine, and Dashboard Reporting & Recommendations. Success = a visitor arranges a 45-minute business review with Akash and Ashwin.

## Positioning

RHAD45 handles the everyday follow-through; RHAD's agency services (SEO, Social Media, Marketing Strategy) create the demand. One agency covers both, starting from a review of the business's own accounts and customer journey, not a software demo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Price: any one RHAD45 module is SGD 100/month. Setup, third-party software and messaging/API usage charges are confirmed before the client proceeds. Never imply all four modules cost SGD 100 together.
- All four modules have equal prominence and are available now (no "Coming Soon").
- Automation starts the follow-through; the client's team stays in charge. Enquiry capture never implies a confirmed appointment; review requests never imply guaranteed reviews or ratings. Loyalty follow-ups are permission-based.
- Form submission does not confirm an appointment.
- Agency portfolio results are RHAD agency credentials, not RHAD45 product results.

## Brand Commitments

RHAD logo (`assets/rhad/rhad-site-logo.png`), RHAD red `#D90A2C`, Unbounded + Plus Jakarta Sans, square geometry. RHAD45 pages use a light theme.

## Evidence on Hand

- Client testimonials (Pisti Prints, SKILLMASTER Training Centre, Fastmoney, Flavor) in `index.html`.
- Team photos in `assets/rhad/` and `assets/img/team-*.jpg`. No photo of Akash yet.
- No RHAD45 product metrics, customer results or case studies exist. Do not invent them. The live RHAD45 page's portfolio logos and case studies are to be swapped in by the web team.

## Product Principles

1. Honest by default: conditional language on price and outcomes, every time.
2. Show the mechanism working rather than describing it.
3. Equal weight for the four modules; the visitor chooses one.
4. One action: arrange the 45-minute business review.
